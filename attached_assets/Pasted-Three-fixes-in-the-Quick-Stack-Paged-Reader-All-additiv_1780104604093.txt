- Detect via e.target.closest(). Treat these as controls: input, button, textarea, select, label, and anything inside the Reader controls (e.g. elements matching '.ocr-edit-wrapper, .read-button, [class*="readbutton"], [class*="ReadButton"], .copy-row, input[type="range"]').
- Use a robust selector. The safest: if e.target.closest('input, button, textarea, select, label, [role="slider"]') is truthy, return early without setting touchStartXRef.
- Page swipes must still work when the touch starts on the plain page/text area (not on a control).
