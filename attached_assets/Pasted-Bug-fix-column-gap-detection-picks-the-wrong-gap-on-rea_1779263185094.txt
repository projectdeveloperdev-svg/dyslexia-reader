Bug fix: column gap detection picks the wrong gap on real pages.

DIAGNOSIS (from device logs, two separate scans of Tucker A&P)

Scan 1:
  midX values: [..., 957, 1058, 1421, 1470, 1725, 1872, ..., 2513, 3061]
  largest gap: 548 between midX=2513 and midX=3061
  splitX=2787, pageWidth=3074
  column counts — left: 48, right: 1, spanning: 14

Scan 2:
  midX values: [..., 1115, 1159, 1561, 1575, ..., 2309, 3030, 3038, ..., 3056]
  largest gap: 722 between midX=2309 and midX=3030
  splitX=2669, pageWidth=3084
  column counts — left: 40, right: 7, spanning: 23

ROOT CAUSE
The "largest gap anywhere" rule grabs the gap between right-column body
text and stray lines floating off the right edge of the page (around
x=3030+). The real column gutter sits between roughly 1100 and 2100 in
midX — i.e. near the centre of the page — but it's a noisier region
with indented or partial lines, so its largest single gap is smaller
than the edge-stragglers gap.

Result: splitX is placed way out to the right, almost everything ends
up in the "left" bucket, and the rest gets tagged as spanning.

FIX (Option B — agreed with user)
Search for the column gap only in the MIDDLE of the page.

1. Define a search band: midX between 30% and 70% of pageWidth.
2. Among the sorted midX values, look at ONLY adjacent pairs where
   BOTH values fall inside the search band.
3. Find the largest gap among those pairs.
4. If that gap > 10% of pageWidth, treat as two-column.
   splitX = midpoint of that gap.
5. Otherwise, single-column.

This makes detection ignore edge outliers automatically — they sit
outside 30–70% and never enter the gap search.
