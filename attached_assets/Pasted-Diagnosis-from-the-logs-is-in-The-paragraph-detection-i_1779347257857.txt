Diagnosis from the logs is in. The paragraph detection in cleanText.js is 
working correctly. The real bug is upstream: ML Kit is returning short junk 
fragments (margin notes, page artifacts, smudges) interleaved into the 
middle of paragraphs. Examples from the logs: lines like "feeing", "nuset", 
"mling", "al or", "nd", "at", "meal", "walkt" appearing mid-paragraph in 
runOCR raw output.

These junk fragments are breaking up real paragraphs and confusing the 
reading order. They need to be filtered out BEFORE cleanText.js runs.

Fix in runOCR.js (NOT cleanText.js):

Add a junk-fragment filter that runs after ML Kit returns its results but 
before the reading-order sort. Filter out a text block if it meets ALL of 
these criteria:

1. The text is short — fewer than 6 characters after trimming, OR
2. The text has no sentence-ending punctuation (. ! ? " ' ) and no leading 
   capital letter that would suggest it's the start of a sentence, AND
3. The bounding-box height is significantly smaller than the median 
   bounding-box height of all returned blocks (say, less than 60% of 
   median height) — indicating a smaller font / marginalia, OR
4. The bounding-box midX is well outside the main text column (less than 
   15% or greater than 85% of pageWidth) — indicating margin content.

Keep it conservative — better to leave a junk fragment in than to delete a 
real word. The goal is to catch obvious garbage, not to perfectly classify 
everything.
