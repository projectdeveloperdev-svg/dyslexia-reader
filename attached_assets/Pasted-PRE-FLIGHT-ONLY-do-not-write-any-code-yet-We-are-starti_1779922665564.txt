Scope of 9.5.1 — camera-quality work only:
1. Tap-to-focus: user taps a point on the viewfinder, camera focuses there. Small animated square appears at the tap point as visual feedback (iPhone camera style).
2. Focus-settle delay: when the shutter is pressed, wait for autofocus to lock before actually capturing. Target 200-400ms. Invisible to user — no spinner, no "focusing…" message. Just a silent pause.
3. Pinch-to-zoom on the viewfinder: standard two-finger pinch zooms in/out. Let the OS / camera plugin handle digital zoom — no custom lens switching logic.
4. Framing guide: a simple rectangle outline on the viewfinder showing the area that will actually be captured. Passive overlay, no interaction.
5. Landscape support as first-class: viewfinder, framing guide, and torch icon all work correctly in landscape. No rotation prompts. No "please rotate your phone" messages.
6. Torch toggle: small icon on the camera viewfinder itself (not in settings). Tap to turn the LED on/off. Persists during the camera session.
7. Max-resolution capture: always capture at the highest resolution the camera supports. No user-facing toggle. Accept the slightly slower capture time.
