PART 1 — CHUNKING (the core)
- Add a small pure helper, e.g. src/features/epub/chunkText.js, exporting
  chunkText(text, maxChars = 1200) -> string[].
- Rules:
  • Target ~1200 characters per chunk (conservative — TTS reliability first).
  • Break at SENTENCE ENDS where possible (after . ! ? and following space/
    newline). Never break mid-word.
  • If a single sentence is longer than maxChars, fall back to breaking at a
    word boundary (space), never mid-word.
  • Preserve order exactly. Concatenating all chunks back = original text
    (aside from whitespace at the join points). No content dropped, no
    reordering.
  • Whitespace-only / empty input -> return [] (that section contributes no
    pages).
- Keep it pure and standalone (no React, no storage). Easy to unit-reason about.
