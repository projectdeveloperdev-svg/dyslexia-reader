STEP 2 — FIX (all three, together):

FIX 1 — Don't speak empty/whitespace pages:
  Before calling speak(), if the page text trimmed to nothing (or has no
  word characters), DO NOT call speak(). Treat the page as "nothing to read".

FIX 2 — A failed/empty speak must NOT trigger auto-advance:
  Auto-advance may ONLY fire on a genuine NATURAL end of real speech. A
  speak() failure (the catch path) or a skipped empty page must NOT call
  onPlaybackEnd / must NOT advance the page. This is what kills the loop.
  - If the manualStop/natural-end logic (Lesson 69 pattern) is the gate, make

FIX 3 — Clean EPUB text before speak (Lesson 71, length-preserving NOT required
here since EPUB has no word-highlight dependency to preserve — but do not mutate
the DISPLAYED text):
  Collapse runs of newlines/whitespace in the text passed to speak() so the
  TTS engine gets clean prose, not raw "↵↵ ↵ ↵". Transform at the speak step
  only; do NOT change what's shown on screen.
